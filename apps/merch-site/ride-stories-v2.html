VYNDI / THE BIKE STORY

Built for the road
you actually ride.

VYNDI begins with the rider and the long road: Indian fit, stable endurance geometry, controlled carbon structure and the confidence to keep going when the ride becomes bigger than the plan. Every kilometre leaves a trace—position, weather, effort, road surface, route and memory—and that same real ride can become a VYNDI Ride Memento, carrying your GPX, photographs, brevet or medal into a personal record of the day rather than a generic souvenir.

Event names and factual details may be referenced. A discovered or uploaded mark enters the memento only after an explicit rights confirmation; otherwise VYNDI uses its own event signature.
What if VYNDI finds the event but not my exact edition?

Select the event, then confirm your edition date, location and distance. The production brief records those confirmed details separately from the discovery source.

What if I do not have the event GPX?

You can still create the event treatment and request route verification. VYNDI should not claim an organiser route as exact until it has been verified for the relevant edition.

Can VYNDI use the official event logo or poster?

Only when the customer or Vāyú Shastr has permission to use that material. Otherwise VYNDI creates an original event signature from factual event metadata.
